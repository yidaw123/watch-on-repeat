The Best YouTube Practice Tool: Learn Guitar from YouTube

Learning an instrument by ear or trying to nail complex choreography? WatchOnRepeat is the perfect practice companion for musicians and dancers.

Transcribing by Ear (The Hard Way vs. The Smart Way)

Learning an instrument by reading tablature or sheet music is great, but transcribing by ear is the ultimate way to develop your relative pitch and musicality. The problem? Scrubbing back and forth on a standard YouTube player to catch a 2-second jazz lick is frustrating and breaks your flow. With our precise A/B loop YouTube feature, you can isolate a specific guitar solo, drum fill, or complex vocal run down to the millisecond. Set your start and end points, and let the segment repeat endlessly so you can keep your hands on your instrument, picking apart the notes one by one.

Building Muscle Memory with Deliberate Practice

You cannot play fast if you cannot play slow. The psychology of deliberate practice requires breaking complex tasks into smaller chunks and practicing them at manageable speeds. Need a YouTube speed changer to figure out a fast sweeping guitar riff? Use our advanced speed controls to slow down YouTube video playback to 0.75x or 0.5x speed. Because our tool utilizes modern audio stretching algorithms, the pitch remains completely stable even when slowed down, ensuring you are still tuning your ear to the correct key.

Active Listening vs. Passive Listening

Passive listening is just having music on in the background. Active listening is sitting down to dissect the structural changes, chord progressions, and dynamic shifts of a piece. WatchOnRepeat’s Timestamped Notes feature is perfect for active listening. As you watch a masterclass or concert, you can add a note reading "Modulates to D Minor here" or "Great hybrid picking technique." Clicking the note will instantly jump the video back to that exact timestamp, turning a standard video into an interactive study guide.

Lightning-Fast Keyboard Shortcuts

We know that reaching for your mouse to drag tiny sliders breaks your flow. That's why we built comprehensive keyboard shortcuts. You can jump instantly between loops, shift the loop forward or backward, and halve or double your loop speeds without taking your hands off the instrument. Plus, upgraded accounts can fully customize these hotkeys to fit their specific workflow, such as mapping them to a Bluetooth foot pedal!
